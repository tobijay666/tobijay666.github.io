Academic Profile

Lecturer & Machine Learning Researcher at NSBM Green University

I am a Lecturer (Probationary) in the Department of Computer Science and Data Science at NSBM Green University, Sri Lanka, lecturing in programs affiliated with Plymouth University (UK). My research centers on Neural Network Design, Explainable AI (XAI), Computer Vision, and lightweight Large Language Models (LLMs).

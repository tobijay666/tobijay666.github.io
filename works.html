Best Track Paper Award (ICARC 2026) 2026
Personalizing University Support: Benchmarking Lightweight RAG Systems Against Commercial LLMs in Higher Education Administration
A. Jayakody, et al.

International Conference on Advanced Research in Computing (ICARC), University of Uwa, Sri Lanka, 2026.
Award Track: Generative AI in Education: Emerging Paradigms, Practices, and Possibilities Track.

This paper investigates the viability of deploying privacy-preserving, parameter-efficient Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) models for academic advisory and student administration services in higher education institutions. The study rigorously evaluates latency, retrieval accuracy, hallucination frequency, and hardware requirements in comparison with commercial proprietary LLMs.

NLP / LLMs 2025
Zero to Hero: Enhancing Zero-Shot Accuracy in Low Parameter LLMs Through Prompt Engineering and User Sentiment Integration
A. Jayakody and T. Perera

Transformative Applied Research in Computing, Engineering, Science and Technology (TARCEST), 2025.

Examines prompt optimization strategies and dynamic user sentiment contextualization to dramatically elevate zero-shot inference accuracy on compact open-weight language models. The framework enables resource-constrained edge deployments to perform specialized reasoning tasks without extensive parameter fine-tuning.

LLM Tuning 2025
User Sentiment Guided Dynamic Hyperparameter Tuning in LLMs
A. Jayakody, et al.

Transformative Applied Research in Computing, Engineering, Science and Technology (TARCEST), 2025.

Proposes an adaptive runtime decoding architecture where generation hyperparameters (such as temperature, top-p, and repetition penalties) are dynamically modulated based on real-time sentiment extraction from multi-turn user discourse, significantly reducing tone misalignment and factual drift.

Evolutionary Computing & Vision 2024
Image Evolution Using Genetic Algorithms and VGG19 Feature Extraction
A. Jayakody and D. R. Karunaratne

International Conference on Advancements in Computing (ICACT), NSBM Green University, Sri Lanka, 2024.

Introduces a hybrid evolutionary image synthesis framework combining heuristic genetic algorithms with deep convolutional feature representation from VGG19. By formulating fitness metrics over latent feature embeddings rather than pixel-space error, the approach accelerates convergence towards semantic representations.
